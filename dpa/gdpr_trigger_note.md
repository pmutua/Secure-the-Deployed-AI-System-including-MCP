# GDPR trigger note

GDPR reaches you if any of these is true:
- EU or EEA persons are among your data subjects, or
- an EU processor or model host sits in the processing path, or
- you offer goods or services to people in the EU

Kenya DPA remains the home statute for AfyaPlus Kenya operations. This
repo makes no claim that its controls satisfy GDPR for any EU-resident
data subject -- see manager_brief.md's "What we will not claim".
